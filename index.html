MORE THAN A FOOTBALL CLUB

Breaking Barriers & Building Leaders in Mirrabooka

Northern City FC was founded to give young people and families in Perth's northern corridor a genuine football home. Working alongside the local community and City of Stirling venues, we create a structured, welcoming environment where players from every background can compete, stay active, and forge lifelong friendships.

  • Social Inclusion: Football accessible to every motivated young player in our community.
  • Pathway for Girls & Boys: Dedicated U13 Girls and U18 Girls squads alongside our boys' divisions.
  • Competitive Ambition: Competing and winning trophies in official Football West competitions.
